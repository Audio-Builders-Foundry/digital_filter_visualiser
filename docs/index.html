Digital Filter Visualiser

an intuitive way to create, visualise and evaluate your digital filter

ABSTRACT


Digital filters are a foundational concept in digital audio Is this working? , but designing them presents certain challenges: from finding the right coefficients to checking for stability.

We present DigitalFilterVisualizer: a tool that allows you to see the mathematical structure of linear time-invariant filters, calculate filter coefficients from your poles and zeros and vice versa, automatically ensure the stability, and instantly hear the result.

DigitalFilterVisualizer features 5 autosynchronized representations of a filter, including interactive visual interfaces, to provide utility in both learning and creating.

A method to convert coefficients to roots is provided using the Francis implicit double shift QR algorithm.
